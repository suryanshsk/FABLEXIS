# Gemini prompt patterns

Every scene prompt must be copy-ready and self-contained. Include:

1. Aspect ratio and intended use.
2. Art direction and rendering technique.
3. Composition, focal subject, depth, and placement.
4. Concrete movement in ordered beats, including start and end states.
5. Lighting, palette, contrast, and texture.
6. Continuity with the previous and next scenes.
7. Negative constraints: no logos, no watermarks, no malformed text, no extra limbs, no random objects, no style drift.

Ask for clean visuals when exact text, numbers, maps, or charts matter. Add precise overlays in post-production. Do not promise a specific duration, frame rate, resolution, or feature unless the selected model supports it.
