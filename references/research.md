# Evidence and research rules

Maintain an evidence ledger for consequential claims:

- `user_supplied`: directly provided by the creator.
- `verified`: confirmed with a reliable source available to the workflow.
- `estimate`: a clearly labeled calculation or timing estimate.
- `assumption`: a creative default that needs confirmation if material.
- `illustration`: invented only as a visual metaphor, never presented as fact.

If research tools are available, verify important claims and record source URLs and access dates. If they are not available, preserve the claim as supplied and flag it for verification. Never fill an evidence gap with a plausible statistic.
