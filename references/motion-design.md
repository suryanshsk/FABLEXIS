# Motion design playbook

## Motion grammar

- Establish the focal point before adding detail.
- Use ease-in for arrival, ease-out for emphasis, and short holds after important facts.
- Prefer one meaningful motion event over several decorative effects.
- Match cuts by shape, scale, direction, or meaning.
- Alternate dense scenes with quiet scenes so the audience can read.
- Reserve camera moves for spatial explanation or emotional emphasis.

## Timing defaults

- Estimate narration at 150 words per minute and label the result as an estimate.
- Hold critical on-screen text long enough to read it twice.
- Use 0.3 to 0.6 seconds for a graphic transition unless the story needs a longer beat.
- Keep a visual hook in the first 1.5 seconds.

## Continuity

Record the palette, type direction, texture, recurring motif, camera axis, and transition vocabulary globally. Each scene must state what it inherits and what changes.
