# Quality-control checklist

Before delivery, check:

- The thesis and hook are clear.
- Every claim has an evidence status.
- Every scene changes the audience's understanding.
- Scene timing is explicit and estimates are labeled.
- Text is short, readable, and safe for the target aspect ratio.
- The palette, type, texture, camera axis, and recurring motif remain coherent.
- Styles vary intentionally rather than randomly.
- Transitions connect meaning, shape, or motion.
- Assets and compositing responsibilities are explicit.
- Gemini limitations are acknowledged.
- No copyrighted logos, copied layouts, invented sources, or false render claims appear.
