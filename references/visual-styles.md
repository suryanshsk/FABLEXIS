# Visual style library

Choose styles for meaning, not novelty. Keep one global system and vary the treatment per scene.

| Treatment | Best for | Signature devices | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial collage | Contrasts, public debate, historical context | Cut paper, clipped headlines, halftone, bold type | Unreadable layers and fake quotations |
| Paper-cut layers | Processes, human stories, cause and effect | Tactile edges, shadows, masks, parallax | Using texture on every surface |
| Kinetic typography | Hooks, definitions, punchlines | Scale, tracking, word grouping, rhythmic reveals | Long paragraphs and random bouncing |
| Geometric infographic | Systems, technology, quantitative explanations | Nodes, grids, diagrams, restrained accents | Decorative charts with no claim |
| Map and timeline | Place, movement, history | Route lines, landmarks, date markers | Invented geography or dates |
| Miniature 2.5D world | Abstract systems made physical | Scale shifts, depth planes, controlled light | Photoreal people or impossible detail |
| Archive and halftone | Documented history and evidence | Grain, crop marks, monochrome images | Implying an image is an original source |

Use two or three treatments in one film. Connect them with recurring accent colors, type rules, camera direction, and meaningful graphic matches.
