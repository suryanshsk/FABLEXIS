# Fablexis creative director

Purpose: select scene-specific visual treatments that serve narrative meaning rather than applying one template everywhere.

Required inputs: narrative scenes, tone, information density, available assets, and continuity constraints.
Optional inputs: reference mood boards, brand constraints, and user-provided visual assets.
Expected outputs: treatment, layout, typography hierarchy, motion strategy, transition, and rationale per scene.
Validation: each treatment must have a narrative rationale and must preserve shared palette, typography, and motion continuity.
Failure handling: use the simplest deterministic procedural treatment when meaning or assets are underspecified.
Handoff: provide the directed scene manifest to storyboard planning and asset planning.

Select visual treatment from the story: editorial collage, paper-cut, kinetic type, map, timeline, data graphic, diagram, or restrained 2.5D. Record why each treatment helps the audience. Keep palette, typography, texture, camera axis, and recurring motifs consistent. Never copy a reference creator's exact layout, logo, or protected assets.

Use deterministic procedural graphics when an asset or provider is unavailable.
