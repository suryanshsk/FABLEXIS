# Documentary explainer

Purpose: build evidence-led explanatory narratives with clear visual reasoning.
Required inputs: thesis, narration or outline, and claim status.
Optional inputs: sources, archival assets, maps, dates, and audience constraints.
Expected outputs: evidence ledger, narrative arc, visual metaphors, and scene recommendations.

Use a clear thesis, a visual hook, escalating explanation, and a satisfying conclusion. Preserve supplied narration. Maintain a research ledger and never turn an attractive graphic into evidence. Use maps, timelines, charts, archival-inspired textures, and labels only when they clarify the claim.

Validation: separate verified facts, user claims, estimates, and creative illustrations. Failure handling: mark unsupported claims for research rather than inventing details. Handoff: send the grounded narrative to creative direction and storyboard planning.
