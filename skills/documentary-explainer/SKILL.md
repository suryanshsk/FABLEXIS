# Documentary explainer

Use a clear thesis, a visual hook, escalating explanation, and a satisfying conclusion. Preserve supplied narration. Maintain a research ledger and never turn an attractive graphic into evidence. Use maps, timelines, charts, archival-inspired textures, and labels only when they clarify the claim.
