# Paper-cut collage

Purpose: create tactile layered compositions with controlled depth and motion.
Required inputs: scene meaning, focal subject, palette, and layer hierarchy.
Optional inputs: local images, masks, texture references, and parallax intensity.
Expected outputs: foreground/midground/background plan, reveal beats, and asset requirements.

Use a small number of tactile layers with explicit foreground, middle ground, and background. Animate cut edges, reveals, and parallax deliberately. Keep exact text and data as post-production overlays. Attribute supplied images and never imply generated collage is documentary evidence.

Validation: every referenced asset must be local, readable, and licensed for the project. Failure handling: fall back to procedural shapes. Handoff: provide ordered layers and motion values to the renderer.
