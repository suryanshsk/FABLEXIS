# Fablexis project

## Creative direction

- Thesis: Ideas become visible.
- Estimated duration: 15.6 seconds
- Palette: #0B1020, #F4EBDD, #F15A4A, #28B8A6, #F4C95D

## Storyboard

### scene-01 (0.0s - 3.0s)
**Narration:** Ideas become visible.
**Treatment:** kinetic typography - the opening hook needs immediate emphasis and a readable reveal
**Visual:** Make the idea 'Ideas become visible.' tangible using kinetic typography
**Motion:** reveal the focal subject, introduce one supporting element, then resolve into a clear graphic match
**Prompt:** Create a 9:16 editorial explainer scene in kinetic typography style. Global palette: #0B1020, #F4EBDD, #F15A4A, #28B8A6, #F4C95D. Show Make the idea 'Ideas become visible.' tangible using kinetic typography with one dominant focal subject in the lower third, with supporting labels in a quiet margin. Animate in this order: reveal the focal subject, introduce one supporting element, then resolve into a clear graphic match. Use a restrained push-in with a lateral parallax shift camera movement. Start with the described quiet setup and end on the focal reveal that leads into the next scene. Maintain the shared a thin coral route line that becomes a reveal mask motif and subtle paper grain with restrained halftone accents texture. Use clean shapes and controlled contrast. No logos, watermarks, unrelated objects, malformed typography, invented numbers, visual clutter, or style drift. If exact text or data is required, leave a clean overlay area for post-production.
